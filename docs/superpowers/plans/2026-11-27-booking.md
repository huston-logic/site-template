# Plan: booking

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-booking-design.md` (rulings R1 to R18). One branch `feat/booking`, one PR.
Opus plans, writes the contracts and the shared plumbing, integrates and reviews; Sonnet implements A and B in parallel
worktrees, then C, D and E in parallel worktrees; Haiku drafts F. Every task: tests first, `npm run check:fast` in its
own worktree before each commit (the pre-commit hook checks the main checkout, which stays clean while agents work).

Contract: `src/booking/schema.ts` (types, limits, timing, refusal messages). Nothing may change its exported names
without the integrator.

## A. Shared rules, `src/booking/` (Sonnet, worktree)

Import-free with explicit `.ts` extensions (the Worker imports them). Tests beside each file (`src/booking/*.test.ts`,
added to `test:worker`).
1. `zone.ts`: `zonedToUtc(date, time, tz): number | null` (null in the spring-forward gap; the first instant when a time
   happens twice), `localParts(ms, tz): { date, time, weekday }`, `addDays(date, n)`, `daysBetween(a, b)`,
   `weekdayOf(date)`, `zoneName(tz, ms)` ("Central Time" style, from `Intl`), `formatLocal(ms, tz)` for emails.
   Tests: America/Chicago, New_York, Denver, Los_Angeles, Phoenix (no DST) on both sides of 2026-03-08 and 2026-11-01.
2. `hours.ts`: `effectiveHours(site, service)` (R6: keys replace, `closed` adds) and `isClosed(date, hours)`.
3. `slots.ts`: `startsOn(date, hours, minutes, tz): number[]` (every N from each window's opening, or the fixed list;
   the session fits one window; gap times dropped); `openDays(input): { date, starts: number[] }[]` for session `index`
   given the earlier chosen starts, busy intervals, now, first date and day count (R4, R6, R8: notice and ahead for the
   first session, the `between` window for later ones, buffer around busy and around the chosen sessions);
   `checkSelection(settings, service, party, starts, busy, now, tz)` returning `{ ok: true }` or
   `{ ok: false, reason: "service" | "party" | "count" | "notice" | "ahead" | "hours" | "closed" | "rule" | "busy", index }`.
4. `price.ts`: `bookingPrice(service, party): { totalMinor, dueNowMinor, dueLaterMinor, perPerson } | null` (R5; minor
   units, percent rounded to the cent, deposit capped at the total).
5. `validate.ts`: `validateBooking(data, { calendarOn, paymentsOn }): string[]` in the owner's words (every limit in
   `schema.ts`, unique ids, windows sorted and non-overlapping, `between` required with 2+ sessions and null with 1,
   price needed for full and deposit, deposit under the price, taking needs a live service), and
   `liveServices(settings)`.
6. `example.ts`: showcase settings (taking on; a one-hour free consultation; a two-session workshop for 1 to 6 people,
   $150 per person, 25 percent deposit, 2 to 10 days apart).
7. `src/content/booking.json`: taking false, the template hours (weekdays 9 to 5, every 30 minutes, 15-minute buffer,
   24 hours' notice, 60 days ahead), one consultation service, `[[placeholder]]` policy, change and gift card wording.
8. Owner sayings as validation tests on edited copies of the file: "workshops need 14 days' notice", "close December 24
   to January 2", "add a free one-hour consultation, weekdays 10 to 4", "take a $100 deposit instead of full
   payment", "the second session must be 2 to 10 days after the first".

## B. Calendar and lock, `worker/` (Sonnet, worktree, parallel with A)

1. `worker/google-auth.ts`: the service-account JWT and token exchange moved out of `worker/sheets.ts`, with the scope as
   a parameter and the token cached per account and scope; `sheets.ts` imports it and keeps its behaviour and tests.
2. `worker/booking/calendar.ts`: a Calendar v3 client over `fetch`: `freeBusy(ids, from, to)` (60-day pieces, any calendar
   error fails), `listHolds(calendarId, from, to, site)` (private property `hl_kind=hold`), `insertEvent` (Worker-chosen
   id; 409 reported as such), `getEvent` (null when missing or cancelled), `patchEvent`, `deleteEvent` (404 and 410 are
   success). Fixed log events only. Tests with a mocked `fetch`.
3. `worker/booking/lock.ts`: `LockCore` (lease of 60 s, a queue, claims kept 10 minutes, `acquire()` and
   `release(token, claims)`), `memoryLock()` for tests and `durableLock(namespace)`; `worker/booking/lock-object.ts`:
   the `BookingLock` Durable Object, a thin wrapper over `LockCore` (imports `cloudflare:workers`, so only
   `worker/index.ts` imports it). Tests on `LockCore` and `memoryLock` (two callers, one waits; an expired lease frees).
